\textbf{TL;DR}\\[0.3em]
The allocation-reduction effort has cut warm 2-file MTAC total allocation
\textbf{146.94\,GB $\rightarrow$ 58.10\,GB ($-60\%$)} and runtime
\textbf{101.95\,s $\rightarrow$ 91.30\,s ($-10\%$)}, with results bit-identical.
The two heavily-optimized phases (Main Search, Precursor Scoring) are no longer
dwarfing everything, which \emph{promotes} three previously-ignored phases ---
\textbf{Quantification \& Output, Protein Scoring, Spectral Library Loading} ---
to the top of the remaining list. The two largest reusable per-thread buffers
(fragment-index scratch, scored-PSM buffer) are \emph{already} reused across
files; they are not the remaining allocators. The best next targets are the
\textbf{serial $O(\text{library})$ map-builds} in Quant \& Output and Protein
Scoring (\(\sim\)3.9\,GB combined, single-threaded, so also a latency win).
}

\section{Methodology \& confidence}
Two independent measurements, used for different things:
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Phase magnitudes} --- Pioneer's own per-phase accounting
  (\code{Mem Alloc (GB)} column), warm run (2nd \code{SearchDIA} in-process),
  2-file MTAC\_3P, 8 threads. This is the \emph{ground truth for magnitude}.
  \item \textbf{Within-phase line/function ranking} --- \code{Profile.Allocs}
  sampling profiler, single-thread, 1-file MTAC, sample rate $2\times10^{-4}$.
  Reliable for \emph{which} lines allocate; absolute GB trustworthy only for
  high-sample-count lines. Single-thread totals do not match the 8-thread phase
  table and must not be read as magnitudes.
\end{itemize}
\textbf{Caveat (read before trusting any single line):} the sampling profiler's
top two leaf frames --- \code{process\_scans\_fused.jl:131} (92\,GB from \emph{7}
samples) and \code{scoring.jl:1459 select\_best\_per\_precursor!} (22\,GB from
\emph{1} sample) --- are low-sample, high-variance extrapolations. They correctly
\emph{point} at the per-scan fused deconv/score loop (Main Search) and best-PSM
selection (Precursor Scoring) as hotspots, but their GB figures are noise: the
phase table caps Precursor Scoring at 7.4\,GB total, so ``22\,GB'' cannot be real.
Magnitudes below are anchored to the phase table, not these extrapolations.

\section{Reusable-buffer status (answered)}
\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{lll}
\toprule
\textbf{Buffer} & \textbf{Reused across files?} & \textbf{Mechanism} \\
\midrule
Frag-index emit scratch & \textcolor{hi}{\textbf{Yes}} & \code{SearchContext.frag\_index\_scratch}, grow-only \code{prepare!} \\
Per-thread counters/int bufs & \textcolor{hi}{\textbf{Yes}} & same \code{FragIndexScratch} \\
Scored-PSM buffer & \textcolor{hi}{\textbf{Yes}} & \code{SearchData.main\_search\_scored\_psms}; \\
 & & alloc once (5000), grow-only, capacity retained \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}
\noindent Both large per-thread buffers live on the per-run \code{SearchContext}
and are grown once to a high-water-mark, then reused with zero further allocation
(Julia never shrinks array capacity on \code{resize!}-down). The residual
Main-Search allocation is \emph{not} these buffers --- it is the per-file
\emph{materialization} of PSM rows (the inner \code{vcat} that decouples the
result from the reused buffer before the next file overwrites it), which is
legitimate output, plus the per-scan work in the fused loop.

\section{Recommended next targets (priority order)}
\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{\code{build\_accession\_to\_species} ($\sim$2.3\,GB, Quant \& Output).}
  Serial, $O(\text{library})$, rebuilt at output time. Build once and cache on the
  spec-lib / search context, or restructure to avoid the per-call dict. Also a
  single-threaded latency win.
  \item \textbf{\code{count\_protein\_peptides} ($\sim$1.6\,GB, Protein Scoring).}
  Same shape --- serial $O(\text{library})$ count. Precompute/reuse.
  \item \textbf{Main Search fused loop ($\sim$29\,GB, hardest, biggest).}
  Needs finer sub-attribution (the 7-sample 92\,GB frame is too coarse). Likely
  split across: transient sparse design-matrix growth, per-scan feature rows, and
  PSM materialization. Instrument with \code{@alloc\_bucket} inside the loop before
  committing to a fix.
  \item \textbf{\code{StandardLibraryPrecursors} getters ($\sim$0.8\,GB).} Check for
  residual type-instability / boxed accessor returns (same class as the earlier
  \code{getMzArray} fix).
\end{enumerate}
\noindent Targets 1--2 are independent, low-risk, and together remove $\sim$3.9\,GB
from phases that have seen \emph{zero} optimization. Target 3 is the big fish but
demands measurement first.
